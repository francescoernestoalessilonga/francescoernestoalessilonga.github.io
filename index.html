About Me

I am a Supreme Court Attorney and Honorary Public Prosecutor with a deep passion for legal and multidisciplinary research. My work focuses on International Law, human rights protection, Sports Law, and the intersections between law, technology, and health.

I believe in an approach that combines forensic practice with academic reflection, providing innovative and authoritative solutions. Explore the sections of this site to learn about my career, publications, and institutional roles.
